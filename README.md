# Bonsai: Self-Improving Feed Filter

Social media platforms actively filter harmful content and optimize for attention and engagement. Still, consuming certain kinds of information on your feed can reduce life satisfaction and encourage poor decisions.

Such undesirable content can be hard to put words to and actively filter because it is subjective and personal.

The goal of this project is to give you greater control over your information diet and make it easy for you to vibe with content you want to see more of while filtering out the rest.
